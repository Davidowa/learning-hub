# Homework 23 · Twenty-six thousand orders, one PivotTable

The take-home twin of Exercise 23, and the largest dataset in the set. A restaurant's orders, 26,609 rows across fifteen columns, which is where a PivotTable stops being one way to do it and becomes the only way. The tasks below are the instructor's own, transcribed from the floating text box that sits over the sheet.

**Objectives** MO-201 4.2.1 · 4.2.2 · 4.2.4

## The data

**restaurant orders**, 26609 rows and 12 columns. [../data/hw23-restaurant-orders.csv](../data/hw23-restaurant-orders.csv)
## What to do

For each of the following statements, generate a new PivotTable. Seven statements, seven pivots, and they stay apart because each layout answers its own question and none of the others.

1. Show the total charged per product, grouped by waiter. Sort the products from the most sold to the least.
2. Show this same report, but in percentage of total income. Sort it by product.
3. Show the sales of each waiter for each of the products, filtered by category. Choose the non-alcoholic drinks, which the data spells `Non-alcoholic beverages`.
4. Show average sales per category per month. The sheet carries a full date and no month column, so the month has to be grouped out of `Date`.
5. Show the profit of each category with a calculated field, and segment it by waiter and by type, which means two slicers on the same pivot. The instructions name the field but not its formula; the sheet carries `Price` and `Cost`, so the profit is the difference between them.
6. Show the percentage, of the grand total, of the sales made by each of the sellers for each of the products. Add a timeline and use it to select a specific month. The sellers are the `Waiter` column, since the sheet has no separate seller field.
7. Create a chart that shows sales per month, distinguishing what was sold by each type, and filter it by customer type. `Type` holds Beverages or Food, and the customer type is the column headed `Client Tye`, misspelled in the source and left that way.

- Restaurant holds order number, date, time, table, waiter, type, product, category and price.
- At this size, check the row count of the PivotTable against the row count of the source before trusting a single number. A filter left on, or a header row counted as data, is invisible in the result and obvious in the count.
- The CSV for this sheet is the largest in the folder. It is text and it diffs, which is the whole reason for exporting it, but do not open it in a plain editor expecting to scroll.
