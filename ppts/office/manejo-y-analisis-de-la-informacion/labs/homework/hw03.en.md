Nothing is calculated: every mark comes from picking the right entry off the Conditional Formatting menu and pointing it at the right range. It lands in week 5, session 9, alongside the sparklines and hyperlinks the syllabus puts there. Hand in the finished workbook as `Homework 3 Excel_Your name.xlsx`.

## Exam routes used here

**2.4.2 Apply built-in conditional formatting.** Select the range the rule applies to, data cells only. Including the header row is the most common cause of a rule that colours the wrong cells, because a text header counts as smaller than every number. Go to the **Home** tab, **Styles** group, and click **Conditional Formatting**. The menu holds **Highlight Cells Rules**, **Top/Bottom Rules**, **Data Bars**, **Color Scales**, **Icon Sets**, **New Rule...**, **Clear Rules** and **Manage Rules...**.

For a threshold, point at **Highlight Cells Rules** and click **Greater Than...**, **Less Than...**, **Between...**, **Equal To...**, **Text that Contains...**, **A Date Occurring...** or **Duplicate Values...**. Type the value in the left box of the small dialog, or click the collapse arrow and pick the cell that holds it; pointing at a cell rather than typing the number is what makes the rule follow the data when the number changes. Open the **with** list on the right and pick **Light Red Fill with Dark Red Text**, **Yellow Fill with Dark Yellow Text**, **Green Fill with Dark Green Text**, **Light Red Fill**, **Red Text**, **Red Border**, or **Custom Format...**. **Custom Format...** opens Format Cells cut down to four tabs, **Number**, **Font**, **Border** and **Fill**, because a rule cannot change alignment or protection. Click **OK**.

For a rank rule, point at **Top/Bottom Rules** and click **Top 10 Items...**, **Top 10 %...**, **Bottom 10 Items...**, **Bottom 10 %...**, **Above Average...** or **Below Average...**. The **10** is an editable spin box, so "top twenty per cent" still starts from the **Top 10 %...** entry with 20 typed over the 10.

For a graphic rule, point at **Data Bars**, **Color Scales** or **Icon Sets**, hover for the live preview, and click the variant. The Icon Sets gallery is grouped into **Directional**, **Shapes**, **Indicators** and **Ratings**; the four-icon rating sets are in the last group. **More Rules...** at the bottom of any of those three galleries opens **New Formatting Rule**, which is where **Show Icon Only**, **Reverse Icon Order** and typed thresholds live.

**2.4.3 Remove conditional formatting.** If the scope is part of the sheet, select that range first; if the scope is the whole sheet, the selection does not matter. Go to the **Home** tab, **Styles** group, click **Conditional Formatting**, and point at **Clear Rules**. The submenu offers **Clear Rules from Selected Cells**, **Clear Rules from Entire Sheet**, **Clear Rules from This Table** and **Clear Rules from This PivotTable**; the last two are greyed out unless the cursor is inside a table or a PivotTable. Click the one the task names. This homework names a range, A2:G36, so select it first and use **Clear Rules from Selected Cells**.

**2.3.1 Create custom conditional formatting rules.** Select the range first, because the rule is stored against whatever was selected when it was made. **Home** tab, **Styles** group, **Conditional Formatting**, **New Rule...**. In **Select a Rule Type** pick one of **Format all cells based on their values**, **Format only cells that contain**, **Format only top or bottom ranked values**, **Format only values that are above or below average**, **Format only unique or duplicate values**, or **Use a formula to determine which cells to format**. The **Edit the Rule Description** panel below changes to match. For an icon set, open **Format Style**, pick **Icon Set**, open the **Icon Style** list, and set each band's **Value** and **Type**, where Type holds **Number**, **Percent**, **Formula** and **Percentile**. For the rule types that colour cells, click **Format...**, set the font colour on the **Font** tab, then without closing the dialog set the background on the **Fill** tab, and click **OK**. Click **OK** again to close **New Formatting Rule**.

![The New Formatting Rule dialog](../../../../img/en/new-formatting-rule.png)

*The six rule types sit at the top, and the description panel underneath redraws itself for whichever one is selected, here a colour scale with its Minimum and Maximum.*

**2.3.4 Manage conditional formatting rules.** **Home** tab, **Styles** group, **Conditional Formatting**, **Manage Rules...**. The **Conditional Formatting Rules Manager** opens on **Current Selection**, which is why rules seem to vanish: open the **Show formatting rules for** list and choose **This Worksheet** to see all of them. Select a rule and click **Edit Rule...** to open **Edit Formatting Rule**, identical to New Formatting Rule. Reorder with **Move Up** and **Move Down**; when two rules touch the same cell and set the same property, the one higher in the list wins. **Stop If True** on a rule stops every rule below it from being evaluated for the cells that rule matched. Change a rule's range in the **Applies to** box without recreating it. **Delete Rule** removes the selected one. **Apply** commits without closing, **OK** commits and closes.

![The Conditional Formatting Rules Manager](../../../../img/en/cf-rules-manager.png)

*Show formatting rules for is the list to switch to This Worksheet, and the list below it is empty in this shot because the sheet carries no rules yet. Applies to is the third column header, and the range it holds once a rule exists is what says whether that rule reaches past the last row of data.*

## Checks

- On `Exercise1`, exactly ten cells in column C are green. Count them.
- Open **Manage Rules...**, set the list to **This Worksheet**, and read the **Applies to** column. Every rule must stop at row 30. A rule that reaches row 31 is colouring the AVERAGE row as though it were a player.
- The rule on column D reads `Above Average`, the rule on column E reads `Below Average`, and they are not the same rule pointed at two ranges.
- The Top 10 % rule on F and G shows 20 in its description, and the Bottom 10 % rule on H shows 15.
- Column J has two rules, one matching `First team` and one matching `Subsidiary`, in two different colours.
- On `Exercise2`, **Manage Rules...** set to **This Worksheet** lists three rules and only three. The colour scale on G and the top-ten rule on D are gone.
- Column D shows four different icons across its cells, not three.
- Change any monthly salary to 15,001 and its cell turns red. Change it to 14,999 and the colour goes.

## Notes on the source

`Homework Excel 3.xlsx` keeps both instruction lists in text boxes floating over the data rather than on an instruction sheet. Two of the items are loosely worded and are read here the way the menu supports them. Item 4 says "values higher than 20% of the data range", which is the **Top 10 %...** rule with 20 in the box, not a threshold of 20 per cent of the maximum. Item 3 of the second list says "a conditional Rating format with 4 ratings", which is an **Icon Sets** rule using one of the four-icon sets in the **Ratings** section of the gallery.
