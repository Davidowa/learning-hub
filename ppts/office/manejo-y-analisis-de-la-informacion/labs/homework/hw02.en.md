# Homework 2 · Managing sheets, cells, rows and columns

Everything in this one is structural. No formula is written, no value is typed, and the whole assignment is about where things sit: column widths in characters, row heights in points, cells shifted rather than whole rows inserted, and sheets copied, renamed, coloured, hidden and deleted. It lands in week 3, session 5, the session the syllabus gives to "add, copy and move, rename, insert and delete columns and rows, adjust height and width, hide and unhide". Hand in the finished workbook as `Homework 2 Excel_Your name.xlsx`.

**Objectives** MO-200 1.3.2, 2.1.3, 2.1.4, 2.2.2

## Exam routes used here

**1.3.2 Adjust row height and column width.** Select the rows or columns by clicking their headings. Drag across headings, or `Ctrl`-click for a non-adjacent set. Go to the **Home** tab, **Cells** group, and click **Format**. For rows, click **Row Height...**, type the value in points in the **Row height:** box, and click **OK**. For columns, click **Format** again, click **Column Width...**, type the value in the **Column width:** box, and click **OK**. The unit is characters of the standard font, not points, which is why 22.29 is a wide column and 30 is a tall row. To size to content instead of to a number, click **Format** and click **AutoFit Row Height** or **AutoFit Column Width**. To change the default for the whole sheet, click **Format**, **Default Width...**, and type the value in the **Standard Width** dialog.

Hiding and unhiding sit on the same **Format** menu, under **Hide & Unhide**. To bring column E back when only its neighbours are visible, select columns D to F by dragging across their headings, then **Format**, **Hide & Unhide**, **Unhide Columns**.

**2.1.3 Insert and delete multiple columns or rows.** Excel inserts exactly as many rows or columns as are selected, so to insert three rows above row 2, select rows 2, 3 and 4 by dragging down the row headings. Go to the **Home** tab, **Cells** group, and click the **arrow on the Insert button**, not the button face. Click **Insert Sheet Rows** or **Insert Sheet Columns**. To delete, select the whole rows or columns, click the **arrow on the Delete button**, and click **Delete Sheet Rows** or **Delete Sheet Columns**. New rows inherit the formatting of the row above; if the task wants them clean, click the **Insert Options** paintbrush that appears next to the insertion and choose **Clear Formatting**.

**2.1.4 Insert and delete cells.** Select a range of cells, not whole rows and not whole columns. That is what separates this objective from 2.1.3, and this homework says so in capitals for exactly that reason. Go to the **Home** tab, **Cells** group, and click the **arrow on the Insert button**. Click **Insert Cells...**. The **Insert** dialog opens with four option buttons: **Shift cells right**, **Shift cells down**, **Entire row**, **Entire column**. Pick one and click **OK**. To delete, select the range, click the **arrow on the Delete button**, click **Delete Cells...**, and in the **Delete** dialog pick **Shift cells left**, **Shift cells up**, **Entire row** or **Entire column**, then **OK**.

**2.2.2 Modify cell alignment, orientation, and indentation.** Select the cell. Press **Ctrl+1**, or **Home** tab, **Alignment** group, dialog box launcher. On the **Alignment** tab open the **Vertical** list and pick **Center**. The other entries are **Top**, **Bottom**, **Justify** and **Distributed**. Set anything else the task pairs with it in the same visit, then click **OK**.

**Sheet management.** Neither exam gives copying, renaming, colouring or hiding a worksheet its own numbered objective, so there is no exam route to quote. The commands all live on one menu: right-click the sheet tab for **Insert**, **Delete**, **Rename**, **Move or Copy...**, **Tab Color**, **Hide** and **Unhide**. In the **Move or Copy** dialog, select the sheet the copy goes before in the **Before sheet:** list, select the **Create a copy** check box, and click **OK**. Forget the check box and the sheet moves instead of being duplicated. The same menu is reachable from the **Home** tab, **Cells** group, **Format**, under **Organize Sheets**.

## Notes on the source

`Homework Excel 2.xlsx` ships no separate instruction sheet. Both instruction lists live in text boxes floating over the data, one on `Estadísticas` and one on `SEMANA 1`, which is why they are easy to miss and why they are transcribed here. Item 5 of the first list says "YOU SHOULD NOT INSERT COMPLETE ROWS" where it means columns; the sense is clear from the **Shift cells to the right** it names immediately afterwards, and it is corrected above. The data and the sheet names are in Spanish in the source and are left that way, because the tasks name the sheets by those names.
