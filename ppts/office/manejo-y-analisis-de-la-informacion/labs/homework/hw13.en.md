# Homework 13 · Cereal nutrition, sorted and filtered

The take-home twin of Exercise 13. Seventy-eight cereals with twenty-two columns of nutrition, small enough to see and wide enough that sorting on the wrong column is easy to miss.

**Objectives** MO-200 3.3.1 · MO-200 3.3.2

## The data

**cereals**, 78 rows and 17 columns. [../data/hw13-cereals.csv](../data/hw13-cereals.csv)

The Data sheet puts its headers on row 1 and the cereals from row 2 to row 78. Columns A to Q are the imported data: date added, name, manufacturer, type, calories, protein, fat, sodium, fibre, carbohydrate, sugars, potassium, vitamins, shelf, weight, cups and rating. Column A is a full timestamp, date and time down to the millisecond, which matters for the third task because only part of it is wanted on screen.

Columns R to V are the answer columns. They arrive empty with their headers already written: `Last 4`, `Code`, `Year - Month`, `Weight-Cups` and `Lenght of the name`. That last spelling is the workbook's, kept here so the sheet and this page agree.

## What to do

The instructions describe the file as a database of well-known cereals, with the manufacturer, the type, the calories and the nutritional values, and then ask for five columns that make it easier to handle. Write each formula in row 2 and fill it down to row 78.

1. Column `R`, `Last 4`. The last four characters of the cereal name in column B.
2. Column `S`, `Code`. Join what you built in R with the manufacturer in C and the type in D into a single code, with a hyphen between each piece. Row 2 is `100% Bran`, made by `N`, of type `C`, so its code reads `Bran-N-C`.
3. Column `T`, `Year - Month`. Take the date the cereal was added to the base, column A, and show the month and the year in the form `2021-July`.
4. Column `U`, `Weight-Cups`. Join the `weight` and `cups` columns, O and P, with a hyphen between them.
5. Column `V`, `Lenght of the name`. The length of the cereal name.

The source says "write a formula" five times and never names a function, so the route is open: `RIGHT` for the first, `CONCAT` or `TEXTJOIN` or plain `&` with the hyphens typed as text for the second and the fourth, `TEXT` with a `yyyy-mmmm` code for the third, and `LEN` for the last.
