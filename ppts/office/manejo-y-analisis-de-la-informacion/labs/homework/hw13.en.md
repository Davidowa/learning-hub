# Homework 13 · Cereal nutrition, sorted and filtered

The take-home twin of Exercise 13. Seventy-eight cereals with twenty-two columns of nutrition, small enough to see and wide enough that sorting on the wrong column is easy to miss.

**Objectives** MO-200 3.3.1 · MO-200 3.3.2

## The data

**cereals**, 78 rows and 17 columns. [../data/hw13-cereals.csv](../data/hw13-cereals.csv)
## What to do

- The Data sheet holds date added, name, manufacturer, type, calories, protein, fat, sodium and fibre among others.
- As with homework 12, the Instructions sheet carries no cell content and the original wording is in a text box. Confirm the tasks before use.

## Checks

Before handing it in, confirm the row count of every result against the source, and make sure any sort or filter you applied is either removed or deliberate. Hand in the workbook as `Homework 13 Excel_Your name.xlsx`.
