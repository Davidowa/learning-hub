## What to do

The task lives in a floating text box on the sheet rather than in a cell, which is why none of it reached the exported CSVs. It lists six steps.

1. Import the information from the file `RealState_Database.csv` and paste it into cell `A1` of the `RealState` sheet.
2. Put the Sales Price column into number format, with a thousands separator and two decimal places.
3. In column `J`, write `Level` as the heading, and classify each operation as `Low` when the area is 60 or less and the sales price is 200,000 or less, `Medium` when the area is 150 or less and the sales price is 1,000,000 or less, and `High` when it falls into neither of the first two.
4. Starting in cell `L17`, show the list of provinces named in these records, without duplicates.
5. For each of the provinces you produced in step 4, calculate the following, and use these four names as your headings: number of operations carried out, average area, total sale price, average sales price.
6. Sort the information by Seller, then by operation, and finally by sale price, from highest to lowest.

- Step 6 is the three-key sort this homework is named after, and the reason to keep an untouched copy of the data before you run it.
- The two bands in step 3 overlap, because a small inexpensive property passes both tests, so the order in which you ask the questions decides the answer. Ask the `Low` question first and let `Medium` catch what is left.
- `RealState_Database.csv` is in the repository, [../data/hw14-realstate-database.csv](../data/hw14-realstate-database.csv), 3,337 records across nine columns: reference, date of entry, type, operation, province, area, selling price, selling date and vendor. It was recovered from the instructor's folder before that folder was retired. The original carried a few hundred empty trailing columns, an artifact of how it was saved, and those were dropped; nothing else about it was touched.
- The RealState sheet is where step 1 lands. In the workbook as it stands it holds a three-row fragment with no header, which the import lands on top of.
- The Sorting sheet starts on row 5 with code, name, last name, date of birth, month of birth, department, position, zone and commission. The recovered instructions never mention it, so whatever it was set for did not survive.
