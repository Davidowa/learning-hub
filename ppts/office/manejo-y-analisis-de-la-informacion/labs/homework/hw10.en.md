## What to do

The task text for the first two exercises is not in a cell of its own: it sits in the wide
column to the right of each block, on the sheet named `Exercise1`, which is why it never
reached the CSV. It is written out here.

**Exercise 1**, the seven vendors in the `ex1 vendors` block, columns Vendor, Trips sold and
Down payment, with `Prize decision` to fill.

> Determine if the vendor will receive a prize, according to the following:
> a) The number of trips sold is greater than 100 AND
> b) The amount of down payments received is greater than $500
>
> If both conditions are met, the vendor will get a prize. If not, he or she receives nothing,
> which the sheet writes as a single space in quotation marks.

Both conditions have to hold at once, so this is the AND case, and the false branch returns
`" "` rather than an empty string. That space is deliberate in the source and it is not the
same thing as `""`: a cell holding a space is not blank, and COUNTBLANK will not count it.

**Exercise 2**, the ten vendors in the `ex2 vendors` block, columns VENDOR, TRIPS SOLD and
STATUS, with `PRIZE` to fill.

> A travel agency will award a prize to its vendors depending on the following:
> a) That the number of trips sold exceeds 25
> or b) That the trips have been paid in full
>
> Complete the PRIZE column with "1 trip to Cancún" if the employee meets one of the two
> requirements. If not, "Nothing" should be shown.

This one is the OR case, and the pair is the point of the homework: the same shape of table,
one rule needing both conditions and the other needing either. Two counts close it, and they
sit under the table as labels with nothing beside them: the number of employees who did not
make sales, and the number who won prizes.
- Exercise 3 carries a thousand deliveries with channel, company, person and commission, which is where a nested IF stops being readable and a lookup starts being the right answer.
- Exercise 4 is the same delivery data cut to twenty-two rows, with an Eligible to Leave column to fill.
- The Características sheet is a five-column truth table and it is the one to solve first: eyes, height, age, works, studies. It is there to be read, not calculated.
