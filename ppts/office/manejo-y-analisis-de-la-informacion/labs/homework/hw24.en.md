# Homework 24 · Farmers market sales, sliced three ways at once

The take-home twin of Exercise 24. Eighty-eight rows of market sales by location, product and season, small enough to verify by hand and structured enough for an advanced filter or a what-if table.

**Objectives** MO-201 2.2.4 · 3.4.2

## The data

**farmers market**, 85 rows and 5 columns. [../data/hw24-farmers-market.csv](../data/hw24-farmers-market.csv)
## What to do

- The sheet starts on row 5 with code, location, product, season and sales.
- Five product families across three seasons and several locations, so every total can be checked two ways, down the column and across the season.
- Autumn is spelled Autum in the data, in every row. Leave it. It is what the file says, and a filter written against the correct spelling returns nothing, which is the lesson.

## Checks

Before handing it in, confirm the row count of every result against the source, and make sure any sort or filter you applied is either removed or deliberate. Hand in the workbook as `Homework 24 Excel_Your name.xlsx`.
