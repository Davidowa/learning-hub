# Homework 17 · Charts that answer a question

The take-home twin of Exercise 17. Two datasets and eight charts, and the point of every one is the type chosen rather than the colours applied. The tasks are the instructor's own, from the Instructions sheet.

**Objectives** MO-200 5.1.1 · 5.2.3 · 5.3.1

## The data

**accounts**, 6 rows and 4 columns. [../data/hw17-accounts.csv](../data/hw17-accounts.csv)

**population**, 33 rows and 10 columns. [../data/hw17-population.csv](../data/hw17-population.csv)

**portal log**, 22 rows and 8 columns. [../data/hw17-portal-log.csv](../data/hw17-portal-log.csv)
## What to do

- Exercise1 is a web portal measuring registered and courtesy accounts. A 3D column chart of active accounts by category, a line chart with markers of internal access over time titled Internal access per day, a stacked 3D bar of press people and guests per day, a line and bar combination with two series for send times against mails sent, and a 3D pie comparing access for company A and company B.
- Exercise2 is state population by decade from 1950. A 3D column comparing Jalisco, Veracruz, Nuevo León, Distrito Federal and Estado de México across the years, a line chart of every state over time, and a map chart of the year 2000.
- The map chart is the one to check your Excel version on before promising it to a group.
