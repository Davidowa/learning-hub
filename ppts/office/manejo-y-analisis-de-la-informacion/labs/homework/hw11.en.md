# Homework 11 · Payroll and service counts under criteria

The take-home twin of Exercise 11. A hundred employee records and a service price list, both answering questions of the form how many and how much, but only for rows that meet a condition.

**Objectives** MO-200 4.2.2 · MO-201 3.4.3

## The data

**ex1 currency rates**, 4 rows and 4 columns. [../data/hw11-ex1-currency-rates.csv](../data/hw11-ex1-currency-rates.csv)

**ex1 employees**, 101 rows and 7 columns. [../data/hw11-ex1-employees.csv](../data/hw11-ex1-employees.csv)

**ex1 payment bands**, 5 rows and 2 columns. [../data/hw11-ex1-payment-bands.csv](../data/hw11-ex1-payment-bands.csv)

**ex2 salon**, 227 rows and 5 columns. [../data/hw11-ex2-salon.csv](../data/hw11-ex2-salon.csv)
## What to do

- Ex1 holds a hundred people with surname, name, gender, date of birth, department, salary, number of children, an additional payment and a total.
- The additional payment depends on the children and the total depends on both, so the sheet is a chain and a wrong intermediate value is silent.
- Ex2 counts services by payment method, cash against card, and totals the price for each. The same count asked three ways.
