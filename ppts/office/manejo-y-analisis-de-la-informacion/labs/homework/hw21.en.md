# Homework 21 · An income statement, five years wide

The take-home twin of Exercise 21. Nothing to look up, and more cleaning than it looks: a real income statement from 2016 to 2020 that arrives as text in one column. Split it, name it, summarise one year, then format it so the loss reads at a glance. The tasks below are the instructor's own, transcribed from the floating text box that sits over the sheet.

**Objectives** MO-200 2.2.5 · 2.2.6 · MO-201 2.2.1

## The data

The workbook stores the whole statement as comma separated text inside column A, one line
per row, not as a table. That is not a defect to work around, it is where the exercise now
starts: fifteen lines of text that have to become six columns before a single margin can be
calculated.

Raw, as the workbook holds it: [../data/hw21-income-statement-raw.csv](../data/hw21-income-statement-raw.csv), 15 lines.

Already split, to check your own result against: [../data/hw21-income-statement.csv](../data/hw21-income-statement.csv), 15 rows and 6 columns.

## What to do

1. Change the format the information arrives in, separating the text into columns. That is **Text to Columns** on column `A`, comma delimited. The header row is `Year` followed by 2016 to 2020, and the fourteen lines below it run from net revenue down to net profit, so a correct split leaves six columns and fifteen rows.
2. Format the information, taking into account that the amounts are money figures.
3. Assign the data array a name, so everything you build afterwards points at the block by that name instead of at a range.
4. Create a summary table that shows the following figures, and only for one given year:
   - Net revenue
   - Gross profit
   - Operating profit
   - Net profit
5. Display a list of the years for which the information in point 4 can be generated. The instructions stop there and do not say how the list is built or where on the sheet it belongs.

The last column is the one that teaches. 2020 shows a gross profit of `-27,837,257` and a net profit of `-42,529,087`, so every decision about negative formatting is visible instead of hypothetical. Point the summary table at 2020 at least once before you decide how the money format handles a minus sign.

## Checks

Before handing it in, confirm the row count of every result against the source, and make sure any sort or filter you applied is either removed or deliberate. Hand in the workbook as `Homework 21 Excel_Your name.xlsx`.
