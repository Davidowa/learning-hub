# Homework 15 · Five filters on the same table

The take-home twin of Exercise 15. The same hundred and fifteen employees copied onto five sheets, so each filter is answered without disturbing the others. The tasks below are the instructor's own, taken from the Instrucciones sheet of the workbook.

**Objectives** MO-200 3.3.1 · MO-201 2.2.4

## The data

**employees**, 115 rows and 8 columns. [../data/hw15-employees.csv](../data/hw15-employees.csv)
## What to do

The Instrucciones sheet asks you to apply the following filters, each one to its own copy of the database.

1. Filter 1. Area `Accounting`, position `Accountant`.
2. Filter 2. Last name `Smith`, section `Fax`, area `Engineering`.
3. Filter 3. Last names `Martinez` or `Foss`.
4. Filter 4. Salary higher than `$28,550` and lower than `$79,280`.
5. Filter 5. Select the five highest salaries.

- Filters 3 and 5 are the two that separate a filter from an advanced filter. Do those last and notice what the ordinary filter cannot express.

## Checks

Before handing it in, confirm the row count of every result against the source, and make sure any sort or filter you applied is either removed or deliberate. Hand in the workbook as `Homework 15 Excel_Your name.xlsx`.
