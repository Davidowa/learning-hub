# Homework 19 · Looking a value up instead of finding it by eye

The take-home twin of Exercise 19. TOEFL scores with a band table, and an employee list, both unusable by hand and trivial with a lookup.

**Objectives** MO-201 3.2.1

## The data

**ex2 employees**, 13 rows and 5 columns. [../data/hw19-ex2-employees.csv](../data/hw19-ex2-employees.csv)

**toefl levels**, 7 rows and 4 columns. [../data/hw19-toefl-levels.csv](../data/hw19-toefl-levels.csv)

**toefl students**, 55 rows and 3 columns. [../data/hw19-toefl-students.csv](../data/hw19-toefl-students.csv)
## What to do

- The TOEFL sheet holds fifty-eight rows of scores and the level each one falls into, with the band table beside it.
- Exercise2 holds thirty-three employees with ID, name, location, salary and age.
- Exercise1 is a small nine-row block used for the first attempt.
- A note left in the sheet by the instructor reads si tienen la función, which flags that the exercise was written when not every machine had the newer lookup functions. Solve it with VLOOKUP, because that is the one MO-201 lists.

## Checks

Before handing it in, confirm the row count of every result against the source, and make sure any sort or filter you applied is either removed or deliberate. Hand in the workbook as `Homework 19 Excel_Your name.xlsx`.
