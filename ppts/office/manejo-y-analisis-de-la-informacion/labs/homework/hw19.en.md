# Homework 19 · Looking a value up instead of finding it by eye

The take-home twin of Exercise 19. TOEFL scores with a band table, and an employee list, both unusable by hand and trivial with a lookup.

**Objectives** MO-201 3.2.1

## The data

**ex2 employees**, 13 rows and 5 columns. [../data/hw19-ex2-employees.csv](../data/hw19-ex2-employees.csv)

**toefl levels**, 7 rows and 4 columns. [../data/hw19-toefl-levels.csv](../data/hw19-toefl-levels.csv)

**toefl students**, 55 rows and 3 columns. [../data/hw19-toefl-students.csv](../data/hw19-toefl-students.csv)

![The Common European Framework equivalence table](../../../../img/en/hw19-cefr-table.jpg)

*The equivalence table the workbook carries as a picture, running C2 down to A1 across TOEIC, the two EOI plans, computer-based TOEFL, the Cambridge and Business English certificates, IELTS and ALTE. It is reference material that shipped with the exercise rather than anything you produce, and it is far wider than the band table the lookup actually reads from.*

## What to do

- The TOEFL sheet holds fifty-eight rows of scores and the level each one falls into, with the band table beside it.
- Exercise2 holds thirty-three employees with ID, name, location, salary and age.
The task text sits on the sheet named `Exercise1`, which holds no data at all: it is an
instruction sheet under a misleading name, which is why it never reached the CSV. It reads:

> Instructions for TOEFL sheet
>
> 1. Put a random number between 0 and 300 in the "TOEFL Points" column.
> 2. Calculate the level columns with the functions indicated in the titles.
> 3. According to the level obtained, show in the "Result" column "Propaedeutic" for levels A,
>    "Intermediate" for levels B and "Certified" for levels C.
> 4. Put a conditional format with a yellow background for those who must take an intermediate
>    course and red for those who need to take the propaedeutic course.

Step 1 is the same trick Exercise 7 opens with: generate the scores rather than receive them,
which means every student's sheet holds different numbers and the answer key can only be the
rule, never the values. Step 2 says the function is named in each column heading, so read the
headings before writing anything. Step 4 is written twice in the source, once ending
"propaedeutic course" and once ending "preparatory course"; they are the same instruction and
the same two colours.
- A note left in the sheet by the instructor reads si tienen la función, which flags that the exercise was written when not every machine had the newer lookup functions. Solve it with VLOOKUP, because that is the one MO-201 lists.
