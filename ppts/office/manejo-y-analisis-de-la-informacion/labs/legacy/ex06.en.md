# Legacy exercise 06 · VLOOKUP, nested IF, and IF with AND and OR

A sales payroll for ten people, built column by column from two lookup tables. Five input columns carry the number, the name, the entrance date, the salesperson type and the amount sold, and the other fourteen columns are all worked out: base salary and sales goal pulled from a rate table with VLOOKUP, commission and differences by arithmetic, two bonus columns by nested IF over banded tables, seniority from a date difference, and two yes or no columns from IF with AND and IF with OR. It comes from a previous year of the course, where it was exercise 6, and the current syllabus records no slot for it. The instruction sheet names the workbook to open, `6.1 Excel 6 (alumnos)_Funciones lógicas`, and never says what is handed in.

**Objectives** MO-201 3.2.1, MO-201 3.1.1, MO-200 3.1.1, MO-200 3.1.2, MO-200 2.2.5, MO-200 4.1.1, MO-200 2.4.2. The seniority column is a date difference, which neither exam covers directly: MO-201 3.3.2 names only WEEKDAY and WORKDAY, and YEAR appears in the function glossary without a route of its own.

## What to do

1. Format the lookup blocks on `Tablas` as tables, and format the report on `Reporte` as a table. Routes MO-200 3.1.1 and MO-200 3.1.2.
2. Format the cells to match what they hold, money as money, rates as percentages and the entrance dates as dates. Route MO-200 2.2.5. The source does not say which format each column takes.
3. In F3:F12 read the monthly base salary out of Table C with VLOOKUP on the salesperson type in column D. Route MO-201 3.2.1. The shape is `=VLOOKUP(D3,Tablas!$A$15:$C$18,2,FALSE)`.
4. In G3:G12 work the annual base salary out of the monthly one.
5. In H3:H12 read the sales goal out of the same Table C, third column, again with VLOOKUP.
6. In I3:I12 calculate the sales commission as the amount sold times the rate in `I1`, and in J3:J12 and K3:K12 the two differences. The instruction sheet gives them as `[sold * % commission]`, `difference ($) [sold - commission]` and `difference (%) [sold/sales goal -1]`. Route MO-200 4.1.1 for the reference to `I1`, which has to be locked as `$I$1` before the column is filled down. See the notes on the source about the dollar difference.
7. Apply conditional formatting to the difference per cent column, K3:K12: red for values under 0 per cent and green for values over 50 per cent. Route MO-200 2.4.2, **Highlight Cells Rules**, **Less Than...** and **Greater Than...**, with the built in **Light Red Fill with Dark Red Text** and **Green Fill with Dark Green Text**.
8. In L3:L12 determine the bonus percentage for sales above the goal, with a nested IF over the four bands of the bonus table on `Tablas`.
9. In M3:M12 turn that percentage into an amount. The bonus table heads its rate column `% on difference`, so the percentage applies to the difference, not to the amount sold.
10. In N3:N12 work out seniority in years from the entrance date in column C and the date in `N1`. The instruction sheet allows either a difference between the two dates or the YEAR function.
11. In O3:O12 determine the seniority bonus with a nested IF over the five bands of the seniority table, each band expressed as a fraction of the monthly base salary in column F.
12. In P3:P12 and Q3:Q12 work out the annual total and the net monthly income.
13. In R3:R12 decide whether the employee receives recognition, with IF over AND: the difference per cent is greater than 5 per cent and the employee has more than 10 years in the company. Route MO-201 3.1.1. The shape is `=IF(AND(K3>5%,N3>10),"Yes","No")`.
14. In S3:S12 decide whether the employee receives a thank you letter, with IF over OR: sales greater than 10 million or more than 15 years in the company. The shape is `=IF(OR(E3>10000000,N3>15),"Yes","No")`.

## Notes on the source

- The workbook ships unsolved. Columns F to S of `Reporte` are empty, and so is every cell of the two answer areas apart from the headers. Nothing has to be cleared before the file is handed to students.
- The two constants in row 1 of `Reporte` carry no label of any kind, and both are formatted General, so `N1` reads on screen as `43985` rather than as a date. Every entrance date in column C is stored the same way, as a bare serial number under the General format, so the whole column looks like five digit numbers until it is formatted. All ten entrance dates fall on the first day of a month.
- The instruction sheet defines the dollar difference as `[sold - commission]` while the per cent difference is `[sold/sales goal -1]`, so as written the two columns measure different things. The bonus table heads its rates `% on difference`, and a bonus for selling above the goal only makes sense against the amount above the goal, so the checks above read the dollar difference as amount sold minus sales goal. The literal text of the instruction is quoted in task 6 so the discrepancy stays visible.
- The bonus bands read "up to 5%", "up to 10%" and "up to 15%", which leaves the boundaries themselves ambiguous, and four of the ten employees land exactly on one: rows 3 and 10 at 10 per cent, row 6 at 15 per cent, and row 8 at 5 per cent. The checks treat each band as inclusive of its own limit. A second trap sits under the same four rows, because a percentage obtained by division rarely equals the typed literal to the last bit, so a test written as `K3<=10%` can fail on a value that displays as exactly 10 per cent.
- No band covers an employee who sells below the goal. Three of the ten do, so the nested IF needs a fall through that returns zero, and the source never says so.
- Seniority is offered two ways, as a date difference or through YEAR, and the two disagree for five of the ten employees, since a YEAR difference counts calendar years rather than completed ones. The reference date, 3 June 2020, falls mid year, which is what exposes the gap.
- The annual total and the net monthly income are named but never defined. The columns available to add are the annual base salary, the commission, the above goal bonus and the seniority bonus, and the checks use that reading.
- The seniority table gives its bonuses in words, `Zero`, `1/4 monthly salary`, `1/2 monthly salary`, `3/4 monthly salary` and `1 monthly salary`, so a formula has to turn each phrase into a fraction of column F by hand. The band `From 15 to 20 years` overlaps nothing, but there is no band for exactly what happens at 20 years and one month, which falls under `More than 20 years`.
- The four rates in the bonus table carry two different number formats, `0.00%` on the 0.5 and 1.5 per cent rows and `0%` on the 1 and 2 per cent rows, so the table displays as 0.50%, 1%, 1.50% and 2%.
- Spelling and language in the shipped workbook: `SALES COMISSION` for commission in I2, and the two sheet names, `Tablas` and `Reporte`, are in Spanish while every heading on them is in English.
- The workbook carries no conditional formatting, no data validation, no sheet protection and no charts. The conditional formatting in task 7 is work the student does, and there is nothing in the file to compare it against.
