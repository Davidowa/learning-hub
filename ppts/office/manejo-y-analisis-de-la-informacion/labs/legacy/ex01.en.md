# Legacy exercise 1 · Formatting the grades report

The first of four exercises that share one workbook. Nothing is calculated here. The exercise takes a
raw two-sheet grades report, widens the columns, styles the banner and the headings, inserts the two
identity columns everything later depends on, folds the second sheet of students into the first, and
leaves one sorted sheet called `TOTAL BASE`. Legacy exercises 2, 3 and 4 each open the file this one
saves, so the saved workbook is both what is handed in and what is carried forward. This is previous
years' material and has no place in the current syllabus; it is kept because these four documents and
this workbook are the only record of what was asked.

**Objectives** MO-200 1.2.1, MO-200 1.3.2, MO-200 1.4.3, MO-200 2.1.1, MO-200 2.1.2, MO-200 2.1.3,
MO-200 2.2.1, MO-200 2.2.2, MO-200 2.2.3, MO-200 2.2.4, MO-200 2.2.5, MO-200 2.2.6, MO-200 2.2.7,
MO-200 3.3.2. Renaming a sheet, colouring a tab and using Undo are asked for in tasks 13 and 16 and
carry no objective code of their own.

## The data

`1.1 Excel1 (students) Grades.xlsx`, three sheets. The workbook ships as plain data: no formulas
anywhere, no defined names, no merged cells, no conditional formatting, no data validation, no sheet
protection, no charts and no frozen panes. Every one of those is something the four exercises ask the
student to create.

## What to do

1. Open `1.1 Excel1 (students) Grades.xlsx`.
2. Widen the columns whose contents do not fit, and set the row height while you are in the same
   place. Route MO-200 1.3.2. The source opens with the observation that some columns show `####`;
   see the notes for why that is not what the shipped file does.
3. Column B is now wide enough to break the look of the banner, which the source describes as the
   title no longer being able to occupy `B` to `O`. Lay the banner across the width of the table. The
   task is written to force the difference between **Merge & Center** and **Center Across Selection**
   to be understood, not just used. Route MO-200 2.2.1.
4. Apply a cell style to the banner, the light blue `40% - Accent1`, and give it thick borders.
   Route MO-200 2.2.7.
5. In `A5` and nowhere else, set borders, 12-point Arial and blue text from the Format Cells dialog
   box. Route MO-200 2.2.6. The source notes that fill is worth showing in the same dialog.
6. Copy the formatting of `A5` to the rest of the cells with the Format Painter. Route MO-200 2.2.3.
7. Make the first ten headings wrap onto more than one line inside their own column width.
   Route MO-200 2.2.4.
8. Set the headings after those ten at an angle. Route MO-200 2.2.2. The source says "the following
   headings" and names neither the headings nor the angle.
9. Insert two columns before `Name`, headed `Num` and `ID`. Route MO-200 2.1.3, and the source asks
   for the Insert command in the Cells group to be shown as well as the right-click route. Every
   column moves two letters right from this point on: `Name` becomes `C` and `Final grade (adj)`
   becomes `T`.
10. Put a consecutive number down the `Num` column. Route MO-200 2.1.2.
11. Move the `Base2` records into `Base1` without carrying their formatting across: cut, then paste
    as values. Route MO-200 2.1.1. The source asks for copy and the other Paste Special options to be
    explained at the same time.
12. Check that the pasted rows now look like the rows above them, copying the format of a whole row
    onto them if they do not. Route MO-200 2.2.3.
13. Rename `Base1` to `TOTAL BASE` and set its tab colour to red.
14. Find `Marcadotecnia` in the `Program` column and replace it. Route MO-200 1.2.1. The source does
    not say what to replace it with; `Mercadotecnia` is the correct spelling.
15. Sort everything by surname and then by first name. Route MO-200 3.3.2.
16. The `Num` column no longer reads 1, 2, 3 after that sort. Undo, then either fill the numbers again
    afterwards or keep `Num` out of the sort by continuing with the current selection when the Sort
    Warning appears.
17. Show every grade to two decimal places. Route MO-200 2.2.5.
18. Freeze the pane so the banner, the headings and the student's name stay on screen while the sheet
    scrolls. Route MO-200 1.4.3.

## Checks

- After task 11 the sheet holds 54 students on rows 5 to 58, with the banner, the weight row and the
  headings unchanged above them. The cut in task 11 empties `Base2`, and the emptied sheet stays in the
  workbook until legacy exercise 4 deletes it.
- `Lab Pmt` reads `SI` in 34 rows and is blank in 20 (23 and 13 in `Base1`, 11 and 7 in `Base2`).
- `Gender` reads `M` in 31 rows and `F` in 23.
- `Program`, counted before any correction, reads `Negocios` 18 times, `Finanzas` 17, `Marcadotecnia`
  6, `Merca` 6, `RRHH` 5 and `Conta` 2. After task 14 no cell reads `Marcadotecnia`. `Merca` and
  `Conta` come from `Base2` and the source never asks for those to be fixed.
- After task 15 the first three rows are `Aguilera, Cristina`, `Alegre, Macarena` and
  `Alvarez, Eustorgio`, and the last three are `Tapia, Ricardo`, `Tovar, Juvenal` and `Villa, Tamara`.
  Excel sorts accented letters by the editing language, so a build set to a different locale can order
  `Álvarez` and `Alvarez` differently.
- After task 16 the `Num` column reads 1 to 54 straight down.
- After task 17 a grade of 8 displays as `8.00`. Read the formula bar as well: the value must still be
  8, because this is a number format and not a rounding.
- After task 18 the split line sits below row 4 and to the right of the name columns, and scrolling
  down keeps both the banner and the names in view.

## Notes on the source

- The instruction file is a table of Comment and Action pairs written for whoever is teaching, not for
  the student. Several rows are asides to the instructor, such as "explain also copy" and "take the
  opportunity to explain UNDO". They are kept above because they say what each step is meant to teach.
- Task 2 is not reproducible in the file as shipped. Only three column widths are set anywhere in the
  workbook, `D` and `N` on `Base1` at 3.29 characters and `D` on `Base2` at 10.86, and neither of the
  narrow ones holds a number long enough to show `####`: `D` holds the text `SI`, which truncates
  rather than filling with hashes, and `N` is empty. Either the file was rebuilt after the document
  was written, or the hashes were produced live in class by narrowing a column first.
- Both banners read `First semester general student's grades report of the 2028-2029 Financial Tools
  course ` with a trailing space, in `A1` of `Base1` and `A1` of `Base2`.
- `Base2` has no `Homeworks` column. Pasting whole rows from it into `Base1` puts `Theory Grade` under
  `Homeworks` and every column after it one place to the left of where it belongs. The paste in task
  11 has to be done in two pieces, or `Base2` has to have a column inserted first.
- The two sheets do not spell the programs the same way. `Base1` uses `Finanzas`, `Negocios`, `RRHH`
  and `Marcadotecnia`; `Base2` uses `Finanzas`, `Negocios`, `RRHH`, `Merca` and `Conta`. Nothing in
  the four exercises reconciles them, and legacy exercise 3 then asks for a list of programs, which
  will come back with six entries for four programs.
- The blank cells in `Lab Pmt` are empty text rather than empty cells, in all 20 of them. That is
  invisible on screen and it changes the answer in legacy exercise 3, task 11, where `ISBLANK`,
  `COUNTA` and `COUNTBLANK` do not agree about a cell holding empty text. Check the three of them on
  the lab build before grading that task.
- Three values carry a trailing space, consistently in every sheet that holds them: the first names
  `Angélica ` and `Lucía `, and the mother's surname `Flores `. Lookups between the sheets still match
  because the space is in both copies, but anything compared against a typed value will fail.
- The document calls the workbook `Excel 1 (students) Grades`; on disk it is
  `1.1 Excel1 (students) Grades.xlsx`, and exercises 2, 3 and 4 all call it `.xls`.
- The .docx carries one picture, the institution's header logo, which is the same image in all four
  documents. No figure was lost in the conversion.
