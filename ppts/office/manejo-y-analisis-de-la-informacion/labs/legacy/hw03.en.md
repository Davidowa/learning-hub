It is the widest of the five in scope and the one with the most room for interpretation. The workbook is `Hmw3_Excel(students)_Concat.xlsx` and the wording comes from `Homework3 Excel_Instructions.docx`.

## What to do

1. Put a title for the database on row 1. It must be centred across the columns that hold information but without merging any cells, which means the **Center Across Selection** entry of the **Horizontal** list on the **Alignment** tab of **Format Cells**. Route MO-200 2.2.2.
2. Below the title, on row 2, write a phrase reading `Information as of (today's date)` where the date is dynamic and updates every day. That is `TODAY()` joined to the literal text rather than a typed date. Routes MO-201 3.3.1 and MO-200 4.3.3.
3. Insert a column holding the consecutive number of each programme, `1` to `23`, and fill it rather than typing it. Route MO-200 2.1.2.
4. Format each column according to what kind of data it holds, colour the columns, and hide the faculty column. Routes MO-200 2.2.5, MO-200 2.2.6 and MO-200 1.3.2.
5. Complete the `% STUDENTS` and `SCHOLARSHIP HOLDERS (%)` columns. Each one is that row's figure divided by the corresponding total, with the total held absolute, and formatted as a percentage. Routes MO-200 4.1.1 and MO-200 2.2.5.
6. Sort the information by faculty, then by number of students, then by level of English, as three keys on one sort. Route MO-200 3.3.2.
7. In the last column build the sentence `In the program of (name of the program), there is a total of (number of students) students`. It must be produced by a formula rather than typed, so CONCAT, TEXTJOIN or the `&` operator over the programme name and the student count. Route MO-200 4.3.3.
8. Calculate the total number of students and the total number of scholarship holders, and give each of those two result cells a defined name. Routes MO-200 4.2.1, MO-200 2.3.1 and MO-200 4.1.2 once the names are used in task 5.
9. Calculate the same two totals again, broken down rather than for the whole table. Route MO-201 3.1.1, with SUMIF over the faculty column; see the notes at the end for what "divided by program" is likely to mean.
10. Freeze the panes so that the headers and the programme names stay visible while scrolling. Route MO-200 1.4.3.
11. Apply whatever formatting makes the database clear and legible.
12. Extra: determine how many programmes have more than 500 students and an intermediate level of English, and how many students there are in total across those programmes. Route MO-201 3.1.1, with COUNTIFS and SUMIFS.

## Notes on the source

- Task 12 asks for programmes with an "intermediate level of English", but the column never uses that word. Its three values are `Basic`, `Median` and `High`, so the criterion has to be written against `Median`. `Median` is itself a poor label for a middle band and is probably a mistranslation of the Spanish `Medio`.
- Task 9 as written says the totals should be "divided by program". Every row already is a programme, so that reading gives back column `C` unchanged. The instruction is almost certainly a mistranslation of "por facultad", by faculty, which is the only breakdown the table supports and the one the check table above uses.
- Task 5 does not say what each percentage is a percentage of. `% STUDENTS` can only sensibly be that programme's share of all students. `SCHOLARSHIP HOLDERS (%)` has two defensible readings, the programme's share of all scholarship holders, or the proportion of that programme's own students who hold a scholarship. The check table above uses the first, because it is the reading that makes the named total from task 8 useful in the formula. Under the second reading Música reads 4.63 per cent, Enfermería 9.03 per cent and Psicología 3.95 per cent.
- The column header is `SCHOLARSHIPS HOLDERS (#)` on `E3` and `SCHOLARSHIP HOLDERS (%)` on `F3`, with the plural on one and not the other.
- The instruction order does not match the order the work has to be done in. Task 5 needs the totals that task 8 creates, and task 6 sorts a table that tasks 3 and 7 have added columns to. Doing 8 before 5, and 6 last, avoids rewriting formulas.
- Hiding the faculty column in task 4 and then sorting by it in task 6 both work, since a hidden column is still a valid sort key, but the sort dialog lists it by its header and the result is invisible. Sort first if the order of operations is being graded.
- The workbook holds no defined names, no conditional formatting, no data validation and no charts. Nothing on the sheet is solved and nothing is formatted: no fills, no bold, no borders and no number formats anywhere. The seven columns have been widened to fit their contents, and that is the only change made to a default sheet.
