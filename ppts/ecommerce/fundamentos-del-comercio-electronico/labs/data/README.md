# Molienda teaching data

These records are fictional. Monetary inputs are in MXN. Vendor fees and tax rates are supplied arithmetic assumptions, not quotations or product classifications.

Order 1001 contains two coffee bags at 289 and one grinder at 1,490. The total consumer charge is 2,068. For this calculation only, the bags have a supplied 0% tax assumption and the grinder price includes a supplied 16% tax assumption. Net revenue is `578 + 1490 / 1.16`.

Variable inputs are product cost 1,140, payment fee `2068 * 0.035 + 4`, packaging 28, absorbed delivery 90 and expected return cost 30. Contribution is 498.10 after rounding the final result. Round only for display, rather than rounding each intermediate line.

The funnel records entrants at each successive stage. Relative losses use the previous stage as denominator. Cohorts have one complete month of observation, so their repeat-purchase rates can be compared on the same exposure window.
